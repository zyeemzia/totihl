Privacy

The ZyeemZia™ Training of Trainers on International Humanitarian Law course runs entirely in your browser.

  • No account is needed. There is no sign-up and no login.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Completed lessons, your best exam score, your theme and language choice, and the name you type for your certificate are saved only in your browser’s local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clear your browser data to remove them.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses.

Questions: admin@zyeemzia.com. Full policies: policy.zyeemzia.com.

Confidentialité

Le cours ZyeemZia™ de formation de formateurs en droit international humanitaire fonctionne entièrement dans votre navigateur.

  • Aucun compte n’est nécessaire. Il n’y a ni inscription ni connexion.
